> Product: ../../mission.md
> Status: planning
> Created: 2026-11-18 · Roles planned: PM, Tech

## Summary

Aya can add, find and open every client: a searchable list, create/edit with only the name required, and a
client card with contacts and recorded marketing consent. The card's tabs (tattoos / quotes / notes) and its
computed summary are the hub that later epics fill in as they add appointments, quotes and tattoos.
