# Calculator & Quotes

> Product: ../../mission.md
> Status: planning
> Created: 2026-11-24 · Roles planned: PM, Tech

## Summary

Aya prices a visit with the studio's pricing formula: several tattoos per visit, a time estimate she can
override, a custom-design fee, extra needles, a discount or a manual price. She can copy the quote to send without
choosing a client, or save it to a client card, where it stays (quotes never expire) and can later be booked.
The device preview and the API's authoritative calculation always agree.
