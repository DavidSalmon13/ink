# Product Mission

> Customer: [../../customer/overview.md](../../customer/overview.md) — product: Ink

## Problem

A solo tattoo artist runs clients, pricing, booking, sketches and finances across notes,
chats and spreadsheets. Quotes are recalculated by hand, and the monthly numbers for the
accountant are rebuilt manually.

## Target Users

- **Owner (only real user):** Aya, the studio's owner-artist, on iPhone and iPad.
- **Clients (indirect):** they only ever see the locked-down sketch "client mode" on
  Aya's device. They never see client data, the calendar, settings or finances.

## Solution

One private, minimal, warm Hebrew right-to-left app in which everything is linked:
client → quote → appointment → completed tattoo → gallery → report.

- A pricing calculator built on her actual rates (minimum charge, per-minute rate,
  design fee), with saved quotes that pre-fill appointments.
- Income derived automatically from appointments that have passed and weren't cancelled.
  There is no manual "payment received" step, and the numbers are never labelled as
  verified income.
- A fine-line visual identity: cream `#F1EDE2`, brown `#76544B`.
