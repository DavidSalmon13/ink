# Ink — Overview

## Business

Ink is Aya's private tattoo studio, run by a single owner-artist. The studio needs
its own management app (a CRM for tattoo artists) for day-to-day work.

## Goals & Engagement

Build a private iPhone/iPad app, in Hebrew with a right-to-left layout, that runs the studio end to end:

- Clients and client card (contacts, tattoos, quotes, notes, marketing consent)
- Price calculator and saved quotes
- Calendar and appointments (center-screen modal for new/edit)
- Completed tattoos and an automatic work gallery
- Sketch library, including a locked-down "client mode"
- "My Data": income from completed appointments, manual expenses, monthly PDF report
- Email newsletters: consent-based mailing list, scheduled sends, unsubscribe

The app is for the owner only. Client and financial data is never visible to clients.
The full product spec is `AYA_INK_new.docx` at the repo root.

## Success Measures

- Aya runs her daily studio work (quotes, booking, clients, monthly report) in the app
  instead of notes and spreadsheets.
- The monthly PDF report is accurate enough to hand to her accountant.
